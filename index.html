Corporate Fraud Documentary Pipeline

A private, single-user tool that produces long-form documentary videos about corporate fraud cases from public court records and regulatory filings, and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Each video is written from primary sources (SEC filings, Department of Justice press releases, federal court dockets), reviewed and approved by a person before narration, narrated with AI-generated speech, and rendered automatically. Uploads are marked as containing altered or synthetic content.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, set their thumbnails and visibility, and read analytics for the operator's own channel. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone.
